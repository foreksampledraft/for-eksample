About Us

For Eksample is a bi-annual independent inter-disciplinary left magazine aimed at generating creative and critical studentship at a concerning juncture in this violent state of global affairs. The magazine understands the capital importance of revolutionary transformation through collective organisation. We believe that a critical readership is indispensable to the intellectual renewal/transformations and collective imagination that our times demand. This platform provides, at its core, is a space to write freely, think in peace and express without censorship.

For Eksample derives its name from the familiar English phrase "for example," but with the insertion of the Hindi ek -meaning "one”. We welcome essays, criticism, reportage, fiction, poetry, visual work, translations, and interdisciplinary explorations that challenge established boundaries and open new possibilities for thought.

For submissions, queries, or collaborations, contact us at editforeksample@gmail.com.
